⚙️ BACKEND (FLASK)
------------------------------------------------------------

Models:
- LegalCitation: id, citation, journal, year, court, parties, summary, full_text, ai_summary, ai_headnotes, pdf_path, share_count, embed_views.
- LawDocument: id, title, type (Act/Rule/Statute), section, text, jurisdiction, year.
- User: id, name, email, password_hash.

Endpoints:
- /api/login, /api/signup (JWT Auth)
- /api/search_citations
- /api/advanced_search
- /api/keyword_search
- /api/analyze_text (AI summary + counter)
- /api/upload_multi_pdf (split multi-citation PDFs)
- /api/rules (Acts/Rules search)
- /api/get_citation/<id>

AI Analyzer Logic:
- Use text embeddings or GPT to detect:
  - Relevant citations (regex + semantic similarity)
  - Counter arguments (AI text generation)
  - Applicable laws (regex for “Act”, “Rule”, “Section”)
  - Auto-summarize into key headnotes.
