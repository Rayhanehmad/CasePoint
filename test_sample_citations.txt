PLD 2024 SC 1
Muhammad Ahmad v. State of Pakistan

This landmark case deals with constitutional rights under Article 10-A of the Constitution. The Supreme Court held that the right to fair trial is a fundamental right that cannot be compromised. The petitioner challenged his detention without proper legal representation. The court emphasized that procedural safeguards must be strictly observed in all criminal proceedings. The judgment reinforced the principle that every accused person has the right to be heard and defend themselves adequately. This decision has significant implications for criminal procedure in Pakistan and sets important precedents for future cases involving detention and trial rights.

2025 CLC 456
Karachi Development Authority v. Citizens Committee

This civil case involved a dispute over land acquisition for public projects in Karachi. The High Court examined the principles of eminent domain and just compensation under the Land Acquisition Act 1894. The court ruled that government authorities must provide adequate compensation based on current market value when acquiring private property. The judgment also addressed procedural requirements for notification and hearing before acquisition. This case establishes important guidelines for balancing public interest with property rights. The decision requires transparent valuation processes and proper documentation in all land acquisition proceedings.

2024 SCMR 789
Federation of Pakistan v. Pakistan Bar Council

A significant case addressing the independence of the judiciary and the role of bar councils in upholding legal standards. The Supreme Court affirmed that bar councils have constitutional authority to regulate legal profession and maintain professional ethics. The case arose from a dispute over disciplinary proceedings against lawyers. The court held that judicial independence requires strong professional bodies to maintain standards. This judgment reinforces the separation of powers doctrine and protects the legal profession's self-regulatory functions. It also establishes procedures for handling complaints against legal practitioners.

2025 PCrLJ 234
State v. Imran Khan (Criminal Appeal)

This criminal law case deals with evidence admissibility and witness testimony standards in Pakistani courts. The court examined requirements for corroboration in criminal cases and the weight to be given to circumstantial evidence. The judgment clarified that conviction cannot be based solely on uncorroborated testimony in certain serious offenses. The case also addressed standards for forensic evidence presentation and chain of custody requirements. This decision has important implications for criminal prosecution procedures and evidentiary standards. It establishes clear guidelines for trial courts when evaluating witness credibility and physical evidence.

PLD 2023 Lahore 567
Textile Mills Association v. Government of Punjab

An important commercial law case involving industrial taxation and regulatory compliance. The Lahore High Court examined the validity of provincial tax levies on manufacturing units. The petitioners challenged new taxation schemes as ultra vires the Constitution. The court held that while provinces have taxation powers, they must exercise them within constitutional limits. The judgment provides analysis of federal-provincial fiscal relations under the 18th Amendment. It establishes important precedents for challenges to provincial taxation measures and requires proper legislative procedure for new tax impositions.

2025 YLR 890
National Bank of Pakistan v. Aziz Enterprises

This banking and finance case addressed issues of loan recovery and collateral enforcement under Pakistani law. The court examined the rights and remedies available to financial institutions for non-performing loans. The judgment clarified procedures for notice requirements and borrower protections before foreclosure actions. It also addressed the jurisdiction of banking courts versus civil courts in financial disputes. This case establishes important guidelines for the banking sector regarding debt recovery while protecting borrower rights. The decision balances creditor interests with procedural fairness requirements.

2024 MLD 345
Provincial Government v. Educational Foundation

An administrative law case involving government regulation of educational institutions and constitutional rights to education. The court examined the scope of government authority to regulate private educational institutions under Article 25-A. The judgment held that while the state has duty to provide education, it must respect institutional autonomy. The case addressed issues of fee regulation, curriculum standards, and accreditation requirements. This decision establishes framework for balancing educational access with institutional independence. It provides guidance on permissible government intervention in private education sector.

2025 PTD 678
Commissioner Inland Revenue v. XYZ Corporation

A tax law case addressing interpretation of tax statutes and assessment procedures under the Income Tax Ordinance. The court examined principles of statutory interpretation in tax matters and burden of proof in assessment proceedings. The judgment held that tax authorities must provide clear basis for assessments and taxpayers have right to contest valuations. The case also addressed limitation periods for tax claims and procedures for appeals. This decision establishes important taxpayer protections and procedural safeguards in tax litigation. It clarifies standards for evidence in tax disputes.

PLD 2024 Karachi 123
Citizens Welfare Society v. Karachi Metropolitan Corporation

An environmental law and public interest litigation case addressing municipal governance and citizen rights. The court examined duties of local government to maintain public infrastructure and provide basic services. The petitioners challenged the corporation's failure to maintain drainage systems causing flooding. The judgment held that citizens have enforceable rights to basic municipal services under the Constitution. This case establishes principles for public interest litigation in Pakistan and municipal accountability. It provides framework for judicial oversight of local government functions and citizen remedies for administrative failures.

2025 CLD 555
Pakistan International Airlines v. Employees Union

A labor law case involving collective bargaining rights and employment contracts in public sector organizations. The court examined the scope of workers' rights under the Industrial Relations Act and constitutional protections for labor. The judgment addressed issues of strike rights, negotiation procedures, and dispute resolution mechanisms. It held that while employees have rights to organize and bargain collectively, essential services require special consideration. This decision balances worker rights with public interest in continuous service delivery. It establishes guidelines for labor disputes in aviation and other critical sectors.
